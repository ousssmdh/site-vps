01 — Surveillance and guarding

Site surveillance and guarding

Security officers on your premises to monitor entrances and buildings, prevent intrusion, theft and damage, and apply the instructions agreed with you.

Who is it for?

  • Business premises and offices
  • Warehouses and logistics sites
  • Construction sites and temporarily vacant sites
Request a quote: site surveillance and guarding

02 — Fire safety

Fire safety

Fire safety officers to prevent fire risks, monitor fire safety systems, raise the alarm and guide the emergency services, and handle public evacuation and assistance to people.

Who is it for?

  • Buildings open to the public (ERP)
  • High-rise buildings (IGH)
  • Shops, offices and logistics sites
Request a quote: fire safety

03 — Dog handlers

Security dog handlers

Security officers working with their dogs on patrols, site checks and alarm verification: a highly dissuasive officer-and-dog team, particularly suited to large areas, isolated sites and night-time surveillance.

Who is it for?

  • Warehouses, industrial and logistics sites
  • Construction sites and vacant premises
  • Car parks and large outdoor areas
Request a quote: security dog handlers

04 — In-store prevention

In-store security and prevention

Prevention and security officers at the store entrance or on the shop floor to welcome customers, deter and prevent theft, and handle antisocial behaviour calmly.

Who is it for?

  • Local shops
  • Supermarkets and hypermarkets
  • Pharmacies and specialist shops
Request a quote: in-store security and prevention

05 — Event security

Event security

A security set-up for your events: entry control, area surveillance, crowd and access management, in coordination with the organiser.

Who is it for?

  • Fairs, trade shows and markets
  • Cultural, sporting and community events
  • Corporate events and receptions
Request a quote: event security

06 — Access control

Access control and security reception

Entry screening and control, welcoming visitors and contractors, checking access authorisations and keeping registers in line with your procedures.

Who is it for?

  • Head offices and office buildings
  • Industrial and logistics sites
  • Buildings open to the public
Request a quote: access control and security reception

07 — Security patrols

Security patrols

One-off or regular patrols to check your premises: securing entrances, visual inspection, reporting anomalies, with a report provided as agreed with you.

Who is it for?

  • Sites closed at night or at weekends
  • Car parks and business parks
  • Residential buildings and estates
Request a quote: security patrols

08 — Temporary reinforcement

Temporary reinforcement

Extra security cover during sensitive periods: year-end holidays, sales, stocktaking, building works, exceptional opening or closing.

Who is it for?

  • Shops during busy periods
  • Sites under works or refurbishment
  • Companies with a temporary need
