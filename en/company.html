Who we are

Security for your sites, provided by attentive officers, with a contact person who listens.

VPS – Vigilance Protection Sécurité ensures the safety of the people, property and premises entrusted to it: guarding, surveillance and fire safety. We support businesses, shops, site managers and event organisers with a set-up suited to each situation.
